<objective>
Plan 15.03 (VCS-22) ships the public `vcs.refs.matchPrefix(id, prefix): boolean` alphabet-aware short-prefix matcher on both backends. Pure-string per-backend implementation — no closures over adapter state, no exec call, no compute beyond regex test + `startsWith`. The CF-04 throw-on-wrong-alphabet rule prevents the silent-false trap (Pitfall 6) where a caller passing a hex prefix to a jj-backed adapter sees a "graceful no-match" instead of the genuine caller bug.

The 5 rules cross-multiplied with 2 backends produce a minimum 10-case test surface. The test is the load-bearing safety net — both backend bodies are short enough (~15 lines each) that the rule enforcement lives in the test, not the implementation.

Plan 15.03 treats the Plan 15.02 `idAlphabet` property by consumers as the canonical metadata source (consumers can compose regex from it) but the matchPrefix bodies hard-code the alphabet regex inline for clarity and to avoid an unnecessary internal read.
