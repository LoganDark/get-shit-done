## Decisions Made

1. **phaseRoot derivation deterministic from mainRepoRoot + phaseNumber.** Plan 01 locked `ParallelDispatchOpts` to `{plan, phaseNumber, mainBookmark, maxConcurrency?}` — no `phaseRoot` field. Rather than extend the public type, the sidecar resolves `phaseRoot` via `derivePhaseRoot(mainRepoRoot, phaseNumber)`: globs `.planning/phases/<padded>-*` (the standard slug-suffixed layout) and falls back to the bare padded-numeric dir if absent. Single `readdirSync` per dispatch; keeps the cross-backend contract clean.
2. **`baselineOpId` returned as `undefined`, not stripped.** Plan 01 kept the field on `ParallelDispatchHandle.workspaces[]` per D-06 forward-compat. This task honors that by emitting `baselineOpId: undefined` per entry. Future liveness-probe re-introduction can populate without breaking the type.
3. **Inline `validateMainBookmark` rather than import.** UPSTREAM-02 prevents importing `validateRefname` from `backends/jj.ts`. Mirrors the floor (non-empty, no leading hyphen, character class `[A-Za-z0-9._/-]+`) inline. Acceptable duplication for the zero-conflict-surface convention.
4. **Eager (not lazy) agent-bookmark creation.** Bookmarks are created in the dispatch loop after `createSubagentSlot` produces the head, BEFORE manifest write. The fanIn batched-delete path depends on these names being live; lazy creation at fanIn time would race the agent's own bookmark writes. RESEARCH §"What's missing for Phase 9" recommendation (a) flagged this.
